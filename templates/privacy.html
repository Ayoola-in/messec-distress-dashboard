How MesSec uses your data

MesSec holds personal information so responders can find and help you in an emergency.

What we store

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your address, blood group and medical notes, if you choose to add them
  • Your emergency contacts' names and phone numbers. Please make sure they're happy for you to share these.
  • Your location and incident details whenever you or your beacon sends an alert

Who can see it

  • Dispatchers and administrators can see incidents and the details of the person who sent them.
  • A station can see your details only for an incident assigned to it.
  • Other citizens can never see your details.

We share your details with responders only to deal with your alert.

Your choices

  • You can correct your details at any time on your My safety page.
  • You can download a copy of your data there.
  • You can delete your account there. We then remove your personal details and keep the incident records without your name or contact details, so there's still a record of each response.

Questions

Contact the organisation running this MesSec service.
