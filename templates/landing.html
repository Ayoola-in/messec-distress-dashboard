How an alert is handled

  1. A beacon or the SOS button sends a distress signal with a GPS location.
  2. A dispatcher assigns it to a registered station, with the nearest suitable one suggested.
  3. The station accepts it and updates its progress. The person sees each step.
  4. The incident is resolved, and the full timeline is kept on record.
